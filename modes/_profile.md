**When the role lacks strategic influence:**
> "The roles where I create the most value combine team leadership, architecture, and product/business trade-offs. If this position is primarily execution management without technical or strategic ownership, it may not be the right fit."

## Your Location Policy

<!-- Adapt to YOUR situation -->

**In forms:**
- State Bengaluru, India / IST when asked for location or timezone
- Prefer remote, hybrid, or highly flexible work arrangements
- Avoid implying willingness for strict 4-5 day on-site roles unless the user explicitly approves it for that company

**In evaluations (scoring):**
- Remote/flexibility is a material fit dimension. Strict on-site mandates should materially reduce the score.
- Score remote-first roles highly when they have meaningful timezone overlap with IST.
- Hybrid in Bengaluru can be acceptable if flexible and senior enough; score based on commute burden and ownership quality.
- Hybrid outside Bengaluru or relocation-required roles should score low unless the role is unusually strong and compensation supports relocation.
- Only treat vague location language as acceptable after verifying the actual policy.

## Your Deal-Breakers and Fit Penalties

Penalize roles that are mostly people management without architectural, product, or strategic input. The ideal role gives the candidate influence over technical direction, engineering operating systems, and business-aligned prioritization.

Penalize legacy or stagnant technology environments when the JD signals resistance to modernization, heavy enterprise maintenance, or a pure Java/legacy stack. Do not penalize mature systems by default if the role explicitly includes modernization, platform evolution, cloud migration, reliability, or architecture ownership.

Prefer modern SaaS/platform environments involving Node.js, Next.js, Python, LangGraph, AWS/GCP/Azure, Docker, Kubernetes, distributed systems, AI-assisted workflows, internal platforms, automation, or product engineering.
